Reference

Claims on display

These are the claims the museum's rooms cite, with the status each one has in the ledger right now. The ledger has more; read it whole in research/claims.md, where every claim carries its parameters, run IDs, reproduction command, caveats and history.

How to read a badge

  • independently checked a second lane ran a distinct implementation and got the same answer.
  • numerically fragile changes or disappears under a modest change of timestep, resolution or boundary.
  • reproduced seen again from a clean process with the same inputs.
  • observed seen in at least one recorded run; not yet reproduced.
  • conjectured a hypothesis without direct supporting runs yet.
  • refuted contradicted by recorded evidence. Refuted claims stay on display.

A badge shows the first status word of the ledger entry. Many entries qualify it (for example "reproduced, not yet by a second lane"); the full status text is printed under each badge here, and appears when you hover over a badge anywhere in the museum.
