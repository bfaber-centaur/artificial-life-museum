The collection

Four specimens, each with a stable ID, a saved starting state that rebuilds bit for bit, and a dossier. IDs starting S0 are reference specimens from the upstream catalogue; S1 are field finds. Names are the registered ones: no specimen has earned a nickname.

Shared conditions

Unless a card says otherwise: kernel radius R = 13 cells, timestep 1/10 (T = 10), polynomial kernel and growth functions with a single ring, Euler steps with a hard clip to [0, 1], 64-bit floats, and a 128 × 128 world that wraps at the edges. All three field finds are catalogued species carried to a nearby rule {{claim:C043}}.
